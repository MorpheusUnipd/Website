European Rover Challenge

The European Rover Challenge (ERC) is one of the largest and most prestigious robotics competitions in the world, focusing on space exploration technologies. Held annually in Poland, the ERC brings together teams from universities, research institutions, and private companies to design, build, and operate robotic rovers capable of performing tasks in simulated extraterrestrial environments.
